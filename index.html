TECHNICAL DISCUSSION

What information helps assess your requirement?

  • Substrate or formulation type
  • Manufacturing process and operating conditions
  • Target microorganisms or performance specification, if known
  • Required dosage, batch size or monthly consumption
  • Relevant test method, regulatory or customer requirement

Evaluation before recommendation

Antimicrobial performance, compatibility, durability, migration or leaching, worker safety and regulatory suitability must be assessed for the specific application. No universal efficacy or compliance claim is made for every substrate or end use.
